# WoTrade

WoTrade is a complete, responsive trading and investment platform for cryptocurrency, forex, and stock markets. It combines customer trading tools, portfolio and wallet management, payments, KYC compliance, support operations, content management, localization, and a comprehensive administration panel.

The platform is designed as a practical foundation for launching a hosted trading service, operating a white-label financial portal, or building a customized brokerage-style experience. Public marketing pages, customer accounts, trading modules, payment workflows, compliance controls, and day-to-day administration are brought together in one consistent product.

WoTrade is more than a visual dashboard template. Its interfaces represent complete product workflows for customers, support staff, compliance reviewers, content managers, and administrators. The system can be configured around the modules and markets required by a particular business.

[Live Demo](https://wotrade.wopixel.com/) · [Hosted Edition](https://wopixel.com/saas/projects/wotrade) · [Source-Code Edition](https://wopixel.com/sourcecode/projects/wotrade-core)

## Platform highlights

- Unified cryptocurrency, forex, and stock market modules
- Spot, futures, binary-options, and asset-swap trading workflows
- Portfolio tracking, multiple wallet balances, transfers, and transaction history
- Stripe, NOWPayments, bank, cryptocurrency, and manual payment workflows
- Configurable KYC forms, verification queues, permissions, and audit logs
- Role-based staff access, IP controls, account security, and activity monitoring
- CMS pages, reusable sections, localization, appearance settings, and support tools
- Optional AI trading, predictions, market news, expert agents, and earn/staking modules
- Responsive light and dark themes for desktop and mobile devices

## Built for complete trading operations

WoTrade connects the main areas required to operate a modern trading platform:

- **Customer experience:** registration, account verification, dashboard, wallets, markets, orders, transfers, deposits, withdrawals, support, and profile settings.
- **Trading experience:** market discovery, favorites, order entry, transaction history, portfolio values, PnL presentation, and optional specialized trading methods.
- **Back-office operations:** users, balances, KYC, funding requests, payment methods, staff access, audit activity, support queues, and notifications.
- **Business configuration:** appearance, navigation, public pages, reusable CMS sections, languages, email templates, authentication, payment providers, and plugins.
- **Platform growth:** hosted subscriptions, source-code licensing, optional modules, expert agents, predictions, market news, AI-assisted trading, and earn products.
